painted.

The painted miniatures, a 3D Far Treasure and a live sky, in five screens you can play. Drag goods into the hold, fit modules to the hull, turn the star map, call the tug.

  1. 1 · live spaceOne shader draws the ether and the stars. Each star blinks on its own clock. Planets turn under a fixed light.
  2. 2 · painted partsPlatforms, buildings, crates and robots from painted-parts-v1, layered in order. Robots walk and beacons pulse.
  3. 3 · the ship is 3DBlender models after the painted art, with toon light, a grime map and an outline, lit like the sprites. Each of the 5 slots swaps its own module.
  4. 4 · the kit on topYou act on things in the scene. A card opens next to the thing, and you confirm there. ₢ runs up and down.

how a planet turns

Paint the planet flat, at 2:1. The three maps below come from noise in this page. The image model paints the real ones.

A shader draws a disc. For each pixel it finds the latitude and longitude on the sphere, adds time to the longitude and reads the map there. The light, the day line and the rim stay fixed, so only the surface moves under them. Clouds are a second map that turns 1.35 times faster.

calls i made

  • The ship is 3D and modular. Everything that stands still is painted.
  • One light for both. A warm key from the upper left and a cool rim from the right, on the sprites and on the ship.
  • A module is a package. A fit swaps the package into the slot and puts the old one in the hold. A strip needs room in the hold.
  • A card next to the thing, not a panel. The only panels left are the hold grid and the ship's numbers.
  • ₢ is the only currency sign. Galaxy years and ship years are the only names for time.
  • The demo wallet starts at 2,400 ₢, so the rigging flow can pay for mk2. The domain starts a player at 1,000.

open

  • The 3D ship sits on the painted platform through an orthographic camera at 42° azimuth and 29° elevation. Lock that angle for every new sprite?
  • Scale: a robot is 44 px and the platform 820 px here. Take the crate as the unit, one crate per hold cell?
  • Star positions are not in universeData yet. Add x, y, z to the systems seed?
