# exported models

runtime `.glb` exports. the editable `.blend` sources and their build
scripts live in `~/Work/theseus/assets/blender/`.

- `stations/`: `lem-station.glb` and `port-of-many-hands.glb`. the port
  view loads one per station id.
- `fleet/`, `civil/`, `industrial/`, `military/`, `research/`: the 36
  hulls of the catalog, listed in `src/model.ts`.
- `painted/`: the painted fleet, its modules, robots and containers.
- `painted/hangar/`: the hangar fleet, 14 ships and 2 police robots after
  the hangar paintings.
